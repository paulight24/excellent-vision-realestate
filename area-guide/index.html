Good to Know

  • Ridgecrest is in the Mojave Desert — summers are hot (100°F+), so bring sunscreen and stay hydrated.
  • Winters are mild and pleasant — great for hiking and exploring.
  • The night skies are incredible for stargazing; the area has minimal light pollution.
  • Most restaurants and shops are along China Lake Blvd, the main drag through town.
  • The nearest major airport is LAX (~2.5 hrs) or Inyokern Airport (~20 min) for smaller flights.
  • Cell service can be spotty in the surrounding desert — download offline maps before heading out.
